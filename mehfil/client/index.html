About Mehfil

MEHFIL — Jahan Har Mood Ka Ek Gaana Hai is an ambient Indian radio sanctuary. Inspired by vintage transistor radios and late-night music salons, Mehfil gathers listeners under one audio sky.

Full-Stack Real-Time Architecture

Powered by Node.js, Express, high-performance SQLite, and Socket.IO real-time WebSockets. Features live synchronized radio broadcast, audience presence, dynamic mood metrics, real-time Farmaish (requests) queue, and live Baithak chat lounge.
